# gr4 routine role statements, tier-2 batch 3

Each statement comes from the rendered facts for one routine in the mgras.a(mgras.o) member:
register loads and stores, calls, constants and branch shapes at the addresses cited. Addresses
are virtual addresses in the named binary. Where the facts do not settle a meaning, the statement
says so.

- `MgrasGlContextAlloc` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0xeb4
  **Inferred.** Appears to take a context slot from a bitmap word at +0x65c of its argument, guarded by a count at +0x660. It tests the count with a signed compare at 0xebc, scans the word against a mask in a4 and a5 (the bne at 0xed0), and sets the bit back with the store at 0xf0c. The failure exit at 0xf14 returns -1 or a value the facts do not resolve.

- `MgrasGlContextFree` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0xf1c
  **Inferred.** Appears to release the slot that MgrasGlContextAlloc takes. It reads the same bitmap word at +0x65c and the fields at +0x664 and +0x668 (0xf20, 0xf24), then writes the word back at 0xf4c. The return value is not resolved, so whether it reports errors is unknown.

- `MgrasEramBufAlloc` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0xf50
  **Inferred.** Appears to allocate a buffer block for an ERAM resource. It calls mgras_baseindex (0xf6c) and getblock (0xf8c) under splhi and splx (0xf74, 0xfa0, 0xfc8), stores the block pointer as an 8-byte value at +0x20 of its second argument (0xfbc) and a length at +0x4 (0xfc4). It returns -1 when getblock fails at 0xf94, otherwise the length at +0x4. The meaning of the getblock arguments is not established.

- `MgrasEramBufFree` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0xfe8
  **Inferred.** Appears to free the block that MgrasEramBufAlloc created. It loads the block pointer from +0x20 (0x1024), calls freeblock with it and the index from mgras_baseindex (0x1020), and updates the same counter at +0x7dc (0x1018, 0x1040). It returns the splx result at 0x1050.

- `MgrasEramRead` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x1058
  **Inferred.** Appears to read ERAM contents through the board window at offset 0x70000 from the pointer at +0xd0 (ten 8-byte accesses at +0x70080, one 4-byte access at +0x70084, and three 4-byte reads at +0x70004). It walks a table reached through the pointer at +0xc8 with a per-entry byte count at offset 0x2b, and calls MgrasInstallTxTbl_Kern with the table at 0x1090. The error path at 0x12bc calls printf, and us_delay with 1 at 0x1254 is a short wait. The exact register sequence is not established from the facts.

- `MgrasNextEramResource` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x139c
  **Inferred.** Appears to return the next unused ERAM resource index. It loops while the index is below 8 (the compare at 0x13b4) and skips entries whose field equals -1 (the bne at 0x13dc). It reads three words with no fixed base (0x13cc, 0x13d8, 0x13ec), so the table base is unknown.

- `MgrasEramWrite` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x140c
  **Inferred.** Appears to be the write counterpart of MgrasEramRead. It uses the same board window offsets (+0x70080, +0x70084, +0x70004) and the same table at +0xc8, calls MgrasInstallTxTbl_Kern (0x1444) and us_delay with 1 (0x153c), and prints on the error path at 0x1634. It returns -1 or 0 at 0x16a4, and the data it stores is taken from the caller's arguments, which the facts do not show.

- `MgrasValidateEramRes` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x1710
  **Inferred.** Appears to validate one ERAM resource record in an array of 0x28-byte entries indexed by a2 (the scaled index at 0x1798 and 0x18d4). It calls RRM_CheckValidFault (0x1754), calls MgrasEramBufAlloc for the entry (0x1784), and takes the cleanup branch when the field at +0x148 of the entry is zero (0x179c). Its cleanup path invalidates the record with rrmInvalidateRN, unmaps with MgrasUnMapGfx and switches with MgrasCtxsw (0x194c, 0x1958, 0x1968). It returns 0, -1 or the MgrasCtxsw result.

- `MgrasEramManagerTimeout` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x1978
  **Inferred.** Appears to be a timer callback that arms itself. It calls timeout with its own address as the callback (sym MgrasEramManagerTimeout at 0x19ac) and a delay of 0x1770 ticks. The routine body has no other work, so what the callback does on expiry is not in this entry, and the self-reference suggests a periodic re-arm.

- `MgrasMoveDemarcationUp` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x19c0
  **Inferred.** Appears to move the boundary between two resource regions upward. It takes the mutex at +0x7b8 with class 0x19 (0x19e8), reads and frees ERAM entries through MgrasEramRead and MgrasEramBufFree (0x1a2c, 0x1a40), and calls reservelow with the boundary value at +0x668 and the index from mgras_baseindex (0x1adc). It returns -1 or 0 at 0x1b38.

- `MgrasMoveDemarcationDown` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x1b64
  **Inferred.** Appears to be the downward counterpart of MgrasMoveDemarcationUp. It uses the same mutex at +0x7b8 and boundary field at +0x668, calls reservelow (0x1c58), and on the failure path calls cmn_err at level 4 (0x1c00). It reads ERAM through MgrasEramRead (0x1cc4) and frees a GL context through MgrasGlContextFree (0x1cdc). It returns -1 or 0 at 0x1cb0.

- `MgrasInfo` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x1d1c
  **Inferred.** Appears to report device state to a caller. It calls MgrasGatherDynamicInfo with the device pointer at +0xd0 (0x1d3c), then copies the result out to the caller's buffer with copyout (0x1d68). It returns 0 on success or 0xe, which is often read as EFAULT, but the facts do not confirm the errno meaning.

- `mgras_regmatch` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x1da0
  **Inferred.** Appears to be a teardown hook that matches a device. It calls MgrasUnMapGfx with its own arguments (0x1dbc) only when its first argument is nonzero (the beq at 0x1da8 skips it), and calls ddv_destroy_handle on the handle at +0x28 of its first argument (0x1de4) only when that handle is nonzero (the beq at 0x1dd8 skips it). MgrasAttach passes this address to gfxdd_recycle (0x1e5c), which suggests it is a registration callback, but the return value is not resolved.

- `MgrasAttach` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x1e0c
  **Inferred.** Appears to attach the device to the graphics driver. It registers mgras_regmatch with gfxdd_recycle (0x1e5c), prints the diagnostics at 0x1e7c, 0x1e9c, 0x1ef8 and 0x1f1c, and maps a 0x100000-byte region with gfxdd_mmap (0x1f48) using the fault handler gfx_fault. It returns 0, 0xc or 0x16; those values are often read as ENOMEM and EINVAL, but the facts do not confirm that.

- `MgrasDetach` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x1f68
  **Inferred.** Appears to undo MgrasAttach. It calls gfxdd_munmap with the handle at +0x28 of its first argument (0x1f7c) and returns 0 on every path the facts show. It has no error exit in the rendered body.

- `MgrasInitialize` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x1f94
  **Inferred.** Appears to bring the board up. The call order is mgras_baseindex, htp_register_board (0x1fd8), init_sema on a semaphore at +0x748 with a 500-tick sleeper timeout (0x201c, 0x202c), us_delay with 2 (0x20ac), then MgrasReset, MgrasRetraceInit, MgrasInitHQ and MgrasInitGioIntrVectors (0x20c4 to 0x2104) with the MgrasBoards table. It returns -1 or 0 at 0x213c.

- `MgrasDownload` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x215c
  **Inferred.** Appears to download microcode to the board. It maps the caller's buffer with maputokv (0x21f0) and unmaps it with unmaputokv (0x2240) and undma (0x2234). It loads GE11 microcode through MgrasDownloadGE11Ucode (0x2278) or HQ3 microcode through MgrasDownloadHQ3Ucode (0x22ac), chosen by a byte at offset 0x2b of the structure at +0xc8 of the device record. The returns are 0, 0x5, 0xe or 0x16, and the 0x5 value is unresolved beyond that.

- `MgrasStart` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x22d4
  **Inferred.** Appears to start the board after bring-up. It reads a kernel option string with kopt_find and atoi (0x2380, 0x239c), initializes several semaphores and a mutex and spinlock (0x23fc to 0x2488), clears 0x478 bytes at +0x1b8 (0x24a0), and creates a GL context with MgrasGlContextAlloc (0x24a8). It allocates a table sized from the rrm_maxrn symbol (kmem_alloc at 0x25d4), then starts the raster backend, texture manager and retrace interrupts (0x26d8, 0x271c, 0x2754). The failure exits free the context and the tables, and the return is -1 or 0.

- `MgrasExit` in `usr/cpu/sysgen/IP28boot/mgras.a(mgras.o)` at 0x2814

Silicon Graphics Brings Powerful 300 MHz MIPS R12000 Processor to its OCTANE Workstation Line

New Processor Reaffirms Commitment to UNIX Products

MOUNTAIN VIEW, CA (February 22, 1999) -- Silicon Graphics, Inc. (NYSE: SGI) today introduced new high-performance 300 MHz MIPS® R12000® processors for its mid-range OCTANE® visual workstations, reaffirming its commitment to UNIX® customers. The new CPU enables the OCTANE systems to deliver 35 percent better performance based on industry-standard benchmarks at no added cost to customers1. This performance surpasses that of a Sun® UltraTM 60 360 MHz by as much as 17 percent2.

In addition, the company announced aggressive CPU upgrade pricing that is nearly half that of Hewlett-Packard3. The availability of the 300 MHz processor will enable the company to offer customers 25 percent savings on the 250 MHz R10000® microprocessor-based OCTANE systems4.

"As part of its ongoing commitment to UNIX customers, Silicon Graphics continues to dedicate significant resources toward advancing the performance and value of its UNIX products running the IRIX operating system," said Murali Dharan, vice president of marketing, Workstation Division, Silicon Graphics. "The release of the MIPS R12000 processor, and plans for other significant enhancements to our UNIX visual workstations, will maintain Silicon Graphics leadership in the visual computing market."

In a separate announcement, the company also said it will offer customers a credit of up to $7,500 toward the purchase of a new OCTANE when they trade in any competitive UNIX system. Customers will receive an additional credit of up to $2,000 when they trade in Silicon Graphics® Indy® or Indigo2TM systems.

Today's announcement ensures that the company's broad range of customers in the manufacturing, government, entertainment and sciences markets have access to leading-edge technology at a competitive price. Graphics customers, such as AUDI, already understand the unique benefits of applying visual computing technology to real-world challenges.

"The combination of a robust UNIX platform with analysis, CAD and CAE software is revolutionizing the automotive development process," said Georg Weissberger, manager CAD/CAM and data management, AUDI AG, Ingolstadt. "At AUDI, we chose to partner with Silicon Graphics because of its leadership in visual computing technology and dedication to addressing our requirements for reliable, high-performance systems."

Easy To Upgrade - Customers See Improvements of More Than 100 Percent

Fully binary- and pin-compatible with its predecessor, the MIPS R10000 processor, the 300 MHz R12000 CPU can be easily and cost-effectively integrated into any existing OCTANE system running IRIX® 6.5. For $4,500 (U.S. list), customers can quickly and easily upgrade their OCTANE system and, in some cases, realize as much as a 106 percent performance increase.5 Furthermore, the MIPS R12000 processor will run any IRIX application without requiring developers to recompile code.

As a result, users of advanced applications such as Dassault Systemes CATIA® will benefit immediately from these performance gains. CATIA, which provides powerful design, analysis and manufacturing applications to cover the entire manufacturing process, has become widely used in industries such as automotive and global aerospace to develop cost-effective digital prototypes -- an alternative to expensive physical models. Since Dassault Systemes announced support for the Silicon Graphics platform in 1996, it has become one of the fastest growing hardware platforms for CATIA worldwide.6

"Dassault Systemes and Silicon Graphics bring the critical solutions needed in the fast-evolving CAD/CAM market," said Severin Lanfranchi, CATIA Foundation Frameworks director, Dassault Systemes. "By continuing to advance the performance of its hardware platform, Silicon Graphics provides CATIA customers with the most advanced design environments available, enabling fast time-to-market and a greater return on investment. On standard tests used to evaluate CPU performance, the 300 MHz R12000 OCTANE delivers average gains of 25 percent on frequently-used, CPU-intensive CATIA applications."

Double the Cache Size and Main Memory Capacity

Silicon Graphics has also enhanced the OCTANE architecture to improve the application performance of R12000 microprocessor-based systems. The company has doubled the secondary cache size from 1MB to 2MB. In addition, OCTANE systems will be able to support twice the main memory capacity (4GB) as higher density memory becomes available.

Availability, Configurations and U.S. Pricing

The new OCTANE systems will be available March 1, 1999. Prices for 300 MHz OCTANE systems will start at $19,995 (U.S. list) and ship with 2MB of secondary cache, 128MB RAM, 4GB hard disk, 20" monitor, keyboard and mouse. Note: U.S. list prices often do not reflect customers' actual cost. Discount schedules and rebates vary. Please contact your local reseller or Silicon Graphics sales office for details.

The Silicon Graphics Value

As the leading provider of visual computing technology, Silicon Graphics delivers workstations that respond to the specific needs of technical and creative professionals in the company's core markets. With a family of products for users of the Windows NT® and IRIX operating systems, the company offers systems that provide an unmatched combination of features -- such as graphics, hardware texture and video -- and performance that enable the creation of high-quality content, regardless of platform.

Texture and video add a higher degree of realism to 3D models, making them increasingly more lifelike. Users in the company's key markets require these features to generate models of everything from cars to human bodies. Silicon Graphics technology continues to enable true "visual computing" resulting in a reduction in manufacturing costs and, in some cases, an ability to save lives.

SGI Solid IMPACT

From Higher Intellect Vintage Wiki

Solid Impact graphics are the lowest tier of SGI's IMPACT graphics offered both on the SGI Indigo2 and SGI Octane workstations. They include a 13.5MB frame buffer and have 1 raster engine (i.e. are "1RSS" boards). Octane boards support the addition of texture RAM (TRAM) which allows hardware-accelerated 3D texturing.
